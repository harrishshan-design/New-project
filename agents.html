FAQ

Clear answers before your first workflow

Can this work with my current lead sources?

Yes. The workflow can sit around WhatsApp, listing portals, forms, spreadsheets, and your existing RealityGenius dashboard.

Is it only for big agencies?

No. Single agents can start with one focused workflow, while teams can connect more advanced routing and reporting.

Do agents still control the deal?

Yes. The AI handles repetitive work and hands over moments that need human judgment, negotiation, or relationship context.

What does a qualified inquiry mean?

It means the buyer submitted useful contact and intent fields. The score is a prioritisation aid, not identity verification, loan approval, or a promise that the buyer will transact.
