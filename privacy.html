Privacy

Your property search should stay respectful.

RealityGenius uses account, listing, search, saved-home, and contact information to run buyer discovery, agent listing workflows, admin QC, and support requests.

What we collect

We may collect your name, email, phone number, role, saved searches, listing submissions, viewing requests, and messages you choose to send through the platform.

How we use it

We use this information to match buyers with relevant listings, help agents manage approved listings, prevent spam, and improve platform quality.

Important note

RealityGenius is a property technology platform. Final property decisions, ownership checks, loans, legal documents, and payments must be confirmed by qualified professionals.
