# RealityGenius Property Tycoon

## Profile

- Time: turn-based, pause at will.
- Space: discrete 2.5D Klang Valley map with 14 locations.
- Agency: one investor controlling a virtual portfolio.
- Conflict: cash constraints, risk concentration, market events and mission targets.
- Content: authored location data plus seeded event order.
- Outcome: 12-turn campaign with win, loss and restart states.
- Players: solo launch build. Rankings and challenges are interface previews for a later online service.
- Session: 8 to 12 minutes.
- Engagement: accumulation and discovery.
- Platforms: desktop keyboard/mouse, mobile touch and standard gamepad.

## Experience Formula

The player feels increasingly capable because every short turn converts Malaysian market information into a visible portfolio decision and a new path toward a real property discovery.

## Core Loop

1. Select a Klang Valley location.
2. Inspect price, yield, demand, growth and risk.
3. Buy a virtual property or improve an owned property.
4. End the turn to collect rent and reveal a seeded market event.
5. Complete missions, gain XP and receive a rule-based RG Genius recommendation.
6. Explore related real RealityGenius listings when interest is genuine.

## Campaign Rules

- Start with RM1,000,000 virtual cash, 100 Reality Points and three exploration points.
- One acquisition or upgrade is allowed per turn; learning and real-listing exploration remain free actions.
- Every turn collects monthly rent, applies one visible market event and refreshes exploration points.
- Win after turn 12 with RM1,250,000 net worth and properties in at least three locations.
- A portfolio below RM650,000 net worth at turn 12 is a loss; other outcomes are rated as developing portfolios.
- Virtual values, returns and auctions are always separated from real listing prices and real-world outcomes.

## Content Progression

- Turns 1 to 3: acquire, compare yield and learn the map.
- Turns 4 to 7: upgrades, concentration risk and demand changes.
- Turns 8 to 11: auction, commercial property and stronger market swings.
- Turn 12: final valuation and diversification exam.

## External Assets

The launch build uses procedural canvas geometry, CSS surfaces and system fonts. No external raster, audio or icon assets are required, so the asset manifest intentionally contains only its header.
